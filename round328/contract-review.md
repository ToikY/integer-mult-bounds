# Independent contract review of the 451-factor mixed cutoff

**Decision.** The proposed cutoff can change the finite compiler loss with the same native rank histogram. I could not derive the complete physical transfer for this cutoff from the transfer assumed for #7863's particular word. Classify any positive numerical result as a finite schedule result pending that implication. No new ordinary supplier or complete gain has been independently verified here.

**Exact references.** Clear1nsight's #7863 at `b3e4412fe01f07dc58305616fabf96345f49a781`, independently reproduced through #7973 at `ac3df5d5c8889f464f575e32dfea43714abf08ca`. In #7863's `research/p104-bit-451-factor-schedule/`, see `PROOF.md` (“Uniform, cover and stock charges”), `independent-review/PRIME-STOCK-451.md` (“A usable-prime existence bound” and “Stock, width and precision”), `source-7830/bit_certificate.py::product_account`, and `replay_451.py`. The preceding compiler proof is pinned in #7830's `PROOF.md` at `87fbf4f7b35b0f18ab12b5767775a9a424cf0b15`. The 480-factor, six-stage physical proof #7846 at `343f7b1817e26ad3f89d2ddad313b12741d53602` does not supply the additional steps by specialization. #7863 explicitly leaves the transfer for its 451-factor, seven-stage variant and the full nested seed as a premise. The independent review repeats this scope. The five names in the consumer JSON do not quantify over arbitrary resegmentations.

**Frozen comparison.** The new all-paid cutoff is J=10^200·11^251 with θ=17/20, P=53 and uniform loss 2660/H. The old J₀=11^451 used θ=13/15, P=60 and loss 3006/H, with H=1202400000000000. The 107,460 literal paid labels, 20,706 productive targets, 451 ordered factors, actual ambient 134^451, 16^451 retained floor and rational native operators can be reused. The verified original ordinary cap is 0.00387992804354799998 and its complete application reports 0.00387992804346. The signed native crossing on these literal ranks is in [0.003879928046154,0.003879928046155]. This interval bounds the native invoice, not the lower compiled ordinary cap. A genuinely different charged uniform or cover recurrence could reduce a loss while staying below the native crossing. It would need its own admissible ordinary rate and complete outer replay under matching premises. Merely retesting a less conservative decimal point of the original compiled recurrence would not supply a new mechanism.

**A distinguishable next test using the old template.** Leave J₀, θ, P, H, q, every slab, all matchers, and every stock mechanism unchanged. The 451 prime receipt proves 64N⁵/q<2^−46. The same quantity is below (6·10^−14)/4. At d'=6·10^−14 the old H gives floor(Hd')=72, so 2^72>1+d'/2. This leaves a numerical opportunity to price the same complete fallback with a smaller loss than the old 10^−13, giving a covered-rate difference of 4·10^−14. Check priority and whether the frozen delayed-cover theorem actually uses d only at these inequalities. The old fixed-loss component recurrence, even at the native upper endpoint, cannot exceed 0.003879928043555 after its two stated losses. At the old trial C the smaller d' gives a covered rate 0.003879928043588, higher by 0.000000000000033. This is a bound on the specified old proof recurrence, not on all implementations of the physical word. Both component rates must still be carried through their complete outer recurrences before claiming a gain. Recompute the seven ordinary strict gaps, outer audit and directed comparison; this review claims no gain for the observation. An alternative rational sharpening at the unchanged P=60 has (H+1)^6000<2^300575, but the resulting smaller uniform loss by itself is only a bound on the old template and requires the same care.
