Write A=134^451, J=11^451, k₀=16^451, δ=1−t and η=tA^(t−1). For a source s the old concavity estimate actually yields capacity ≥η sᵗ(As/n)^δ after the common majorant and normalization are removed. The source-count denominator cancels. It is enough to lower-bound the t-mass-weighted mean of (As/n)^δ for every group of actual unmatched sources.

1. At any root unit of degree G, c(a)≥k₀^G and n/A<k₀^(G+1) by maximality of G. For target k the source s=k c(a) thus satisfies As/n>k/k₀. The complete productive word of that unit occurs with its common integer multiplicity u(a), giving lower multiplier B_root=ΣW_k k /(k₀^δ ΣW_k kᵗ). This includes G=0 and n one below the next threshold.
2. For nonroot sources fix the preceding paid prefix with column multiplier ρ, the **child** index b, and a target k. Put L=n/(Aρc(b)). A missing parent means L<k; a child corresponding to paid label r is present exactly when r≤L. Its source rank is s=kρr c(b), hence As/n=kr/L. Every admitted sibling contributes the same u(b)W_k source slots per chronological outgoing paid label if the parent index is absent. A present parent supplies the target slots by the ordinary Pascal matching. Check that common multiplicity using the source-allocation formula before charging any numerical benefit.
3. If L<J, the source has As/n>k₀/J even when r=1. If L≥J, the entire paid word is admitted. Averaging over its actual siblings with t-weights rᵗ gives lower multiplier B_paid=ΣP_r r/ΣP_r rᵗ since k/L>1. Equality L=J is in the full-word case. Thus η times min(B_root,(k₀/J)^δ,B_paid) suffices for all G with no attribution of an exit to a source, no assumption of large G and no rank-one exception.
