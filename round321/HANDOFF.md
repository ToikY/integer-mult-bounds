# Rank of three removed deliveries at a shared receiver

On the exact #7786 binary word used by #7830, take any two MOVE deliveries on the same directed route. Choose as the third any earlier MOVE delivery into their receiver, including another delivery from the same sender. Keep the remaining transfer equations fixed. The independent literal forward/adjoint census covers all 115,156 such triples on 246 repeated pair routes. The difference between the intact public transfer and the transfer with all three deliveries suppressed has rank three in every case. Two scalar replacements cannot supply this correction through the frozen exterior. No verified κ gain.

**Proof.** Order the three cut deliveries chronologically. In the intact linear network, let the rows of V be their payloads and the columns of C be their full terminal impulse responses. Let L be the three-by-three transfer from pulses arriving at those cuts to the values presented immediately before those deliveries. A pulse cannot affect its own send or earlier sends, so L is strictly lower triangular. The innovations needed to zero the three arrivals are (I+L)⁻¹V over GF(2). The loss of public transfer is C(I+L)⁻¹V. A full-row-rank V is surjective onto three bits; a full-column-rank C is injective on those bits. The middle matrix is invertible. This proves exact rank three, including any feed-through among the deliveries. Two scalar contributions through the zero-cut exterior have rank at most two. This last conclusion assumes that all other transfer equations, including any direct local-through terms, remain frozen. Joint recoding of another paid line, extra local observations at a changed decoder, non-scalar paths or changing productive endpoints can change that premise.

The independently implemented verifier reconstructs every payload and every response from the original signed-direction and transposed binary literal tapes. It checks both 680-column operators and all 3,554 dirty retirements in each direction. A basis calculation verifies both triple ranks, with a digest over ordered packet IDs and ranks for each route, plus explicit candidate streams. The primary graph-extraction implementation uses a different basis propagation algorithm. The two engines agree on all 115,156 cases. Every in-scope triple was resolved (60 s discovery/30 s independent caps).

**Named case.** An already-paid grade-two arrival 1358 (125→507, clock 2404) precedes two grade-four deliveries 2291 and 3419 (0→507, clocks 7018 and 20938). First receiving ADD clocks are respectively 7029, 7091 and 20947. Dropping none, then successive prefixes of these deliveries, produces measured public defect ranks 0, 1, 2 and 3. Three actual tagged injections on the intact tape match the adjoint pulse columns. Injections on the zero-cut tape reconstruct every entry of the named three-deletion defect. The optimistic gross saving of retiring one grade-four job in each of the 36 calls has no valid two-scalar realization in this frozen model.
