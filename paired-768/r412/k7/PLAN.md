# One-call support at the largest K7 MOVE clocks

Fork handoff source is #8057's exact K7 graph at the #8094 ordinary invoice.
Freeze the first 64 MOVE clocks ordered by descending chart dimension
difference and then by original zero-based clock, before reading support.
At most 90 elapsed seconds, and at most those 64 cases. Test a metadata-only
forward chart change or deletion of the paid call, preserving its register
and all other chart and processor actions. In the full arbitrary-input
register contract, one live normalized basis coefficient and one nonzero
column of the exact projector difference rule out an identity action.
Check both chronological chart nesting and a literal arbitrary-input
counterexample. An exact zero at the input would trigger one literal
source splice, with a separate owner and reverse/restore check. Stop at
the first zero. When all 64 exact cases are nonzero, retire that version
for these sites. Other clocks, correlated caller restrictions, changed
charts and larger programs remain open.

A successful same-owner, same-chart-itinerary replacement would remove
36 equal paid rank calls from the 36 LOCAL copies, with all productive
ranks preserved. The reported gross raw moment saving uses the same K7
binary dyadic trial and an outward integer interval; there is no positive
numerical lower bound on any additional possible repair. A proposed
replacement that has to restore the omitted action through one call of
the same rank loses the full raw moment saving before routing charges.
Every new route, chart change, scratch action, preservation obligation
and storage change would need separate pricing. No alternate restoration
scheme is part of the tested family.
