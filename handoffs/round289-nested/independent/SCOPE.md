# Scope of the two-specialization rank proof

Let T+ and T− be the old chronological 36×36 actions on the two fixed width-one Walsh fibres with all bodies +1 and only B9=−1, respectively. The ordinary square translation for stages9–15 acts identically on these fibres because the only changed body eigenvalue is −1 and its square is +1. The replacement paid B16 operation also has identical eigenvalue +1. The retained rank64 idle on role37 is therefore the only sector-varying operation in the proposed reduced core, and its action difference on the role space is −2 e37 e37ᵀ.

Allow arbitrary induced linear wrappers L and R of compatible dimensions and an additive bypass E, so long as the induced L, R and E are the same on the two fixed fibres and there is only this one varying role action. The proposed difference is R(−2 e37 e37ᵀ)L and has rank at most one. The reverse endpoint check directly measures rank(T−−T+) in the original chronological window. Any rank above one excludes this fixed-fibre reduction even when the wrappers are arbitrary and noninvertible. A sector-dependent wrapper, access to another varying action or physical cross-fibre routing is outside that model. The proof uses neither Fourier-fibre independence of all possible physical routines nor a universal wrapper impossibility.
